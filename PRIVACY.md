# Swell privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026. Swell is made by Keep It Lit Software Inc.

## What Swell collects

Nothing. Swell has no INTERNET permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. There are
no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting.

## What Swell keeps on your phone

- **Launch history:** which apps you open from Swell, how often and when. This is what orders the
  list. Swell keeps it in its own private storage and excludes it from Android backups.
- **Your settings:** accent, colours, clock, icon size and the other choices on the Settings
  screen.

No other app can read either. **Start over** in Settings erases the launch history; uninstalling
Swell erases both.

## Permissions

- **Query installed apps** (through the launcher intent): to list the apps you can open.
- **Access hidden profiles:** to list apps in a work profile or Private Space.
- **Request uninstall:** to uninstall apps you choose, from the long-press menu or Least used.
  Android asks you to confirm each one.
- **Display over other apps** (optional, off until you allow it): Swell uses it only to show its
  own home screen during the second Android spends animating back to home.

## Changes

A change to this policy is published here, at
