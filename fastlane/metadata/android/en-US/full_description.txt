Swell is an Android home screen you never arrange. Home is one list, an app to a line, kept in the order you actually open things.

Every launch is a vote. Votes decay slowly, so this week matters more than last month, but never enough to yank an app overnight. An app passes the one above it only when it clearly beats it, so the list settles into something your thumb can learn.

• No folders, no dragging, nothing to configure
• The apps you use most head the list; the lines stay still while the apps step through them
• Every app, alphabetical and searchable, with a letter rail, one swipe away
• Least used: review the apps you rarely open and uninstall one or several at once
• Accent colours, a role-by-role colour table, monochrome icons
• Widgets, pinned shortcuts ("Add to Home screen" from your browser), and a live wallpaper that follows your colours
• Work and private profiles

Swell has no INTERNET permission. Your launch history never leaves the phone.
